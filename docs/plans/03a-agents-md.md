# Stage 3 Appendix — Complete `AGENTS.md` Draft

**Related:** [03-local-ingest-toolchain.md](03-local-ingest-toolchain.md)

This document is a draft of the `AGENTS.md` content that `gdg wiki clone` places at a clone root.
During implementation, store it as a constant in `wiki/app/lib/agents-md.server.ts` and serve it via `GET /api/cli/wiki/agents-md`.

It is centrally managed on the server so every operator has identical content.
It is expected to evolve in operation; whenever it changes, always record why in the Wiki's `log` page.

---

## `AGENTS.md` content follows

````markdown
# GDG Japan Wiki — Maintainer Instructions

You are the maintainer responsible for the Wiki in this directory.
Your job is to read primary information, integrate it into the existing page set, and keep cross-references and the catalog current.

## Directory layout

```
AGENTS.md          These instructions. Do not edit.
INGEST_QUEUE.md    Items to process this time. Generated by gdg wiki ingest.
pages/**           The layer you write. Git-tracked and synchronized to the Wiki on push.
raw/**             Primary information. Read only; do not rewrite.
.gdgwiki/          CLI state. Do not touch.
```

Each directory under `pages/` is one page and contains `page.md` and `assets/`.
Directory nesting directly represents parent-child page relationships. A directory name must match the front matter `slug`.

### Prohibitions

- Do not edit or delete `raw/**`. Primary information is immutable.
- Do not remove `raw/` from `.gitignore`. The server rejects pushes of anything outside `pages/**`, which would break synchronization.
- Do not rewrite the front matter `id`. It is an identifier assigned by the server.
- Do not create files outside `pages/` (do not leave processing traces in `INGEST_QUEUE.md`, either).

## Page types and placement

The top level consists of namespaces by type. Always place a new page beneath one of them.

| Directory | `page_type` | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `events/` | `event` | Record for one event: date, venue, attendance, budget, and performance by recruitment channel. |
| `venues/` | `venue` | One venue: capacity, costs, facilities, access, usage history, and practical issues encountered. |
| `vendors/` | `vendor` | Vendors such as catering, printing, and novelty suppliers: unit prices, service area, ordering lead time, and history. |
| `people/` | `person` | Speakers and staff: speaking history, contact channel (method only), and how receptive they were to requests. |
| `orgs/` | `organization` | Sponsor companies and partner organizations: point of contact and past sponsorship details. |
| `playbooks/` | `playbook` | Procedures and copy templates: target task, timeline, and decision criteria. |

`index` and `log` are special root-level pages with types `wiki-index` and `wiki-log`, respectively.

Even when information is specific to an event, facts belonging to a venue, vendor, or person must be written on that entity's page.
Link to it from the `events/` page. Duplicating the same venue information per event inevitably creates contradictions.

## Front matter

```yaml
---
gdg_wiki: 1
id: 7sLHj_rsleSlrEd1yH6CN   # Assigned by the server. Do not change. Omit for a new page.
slug: umeda-innovation-hub
language: ja
title: Umeda Innovation Hub
summary: Capacity 120. Equipment may be brought in. Used twice in 2026.
translation_status: human
page_type: venue
page_metadata:
  capacity: 120
  area: Kita Ward, Osaka
parent_slug: venues
sort_order: 10
visibility: restricted
general_role: viewer
tags: [venue, Osaka]
sources:
  - source_id: xxxxx
    title: July 14 regular meeting minutes
---
```

- `language` is fixed to the language chosen at clone time. Do not change it.
- Do not write `id` for a new page. The server assigns it and it appears in the next clone.
- `parent_slug` must match the parent directory name.
- Always write `summary`: one or two sentences shown in `index` and search results.
- `visibility` defaults to `restricted`. Do not raise it without evidence that publication is appropriate.

## Ingest procedure

For each ingest, process **only the first source in `INGEST_QUEUE.md`**.
You may touch at most 15 pages per source. If it exceeds that limit, split the source and defer the rest to the next run.

1. Read the `raw/` path in the first entry of `INGEST_QUEUE.md`.
2. Read `index` and identify relevant existing pages.
3. Prioritize updating existing pages. Create a page only when no existing page can contain the information.
4. After writing a fact, add the supporting `source_id` to front matter `sources`.
5. When creating a page, add one line to `index`.
6. Append one line to `log`.
7. Commit the changes and run `git push`.

### Updating existing pages

You own all pages under `pages/`. You may rewrite them comprehensively.

However, some pages may have been edited by humans. When changing content in a direction that reverses such an edit, record why you made that judgment in `log`.

Human-authored pages do not appear in `pages/`; they appear under `raw/`. Read them as primary information, and separately create a synthesized page integrating their content under `playbooks/` or elsewhere.
The original remains, so cite it in the synthesized page's `sources` and link to it in the body as well. The correct state is for the human essay and practical version to coexist.

### Handling contradictions

When a new source conflicts with existing text, do not arbitrarily choose one.

Put an "Unresolved" block on the relevant page and list both values and sources.

```markdown
> **Unresolved**: capacity
> - 120 people — July 14 regular meeting minutes (`source_id: xxxxx`)
> - 90 people — Google Chat #io-extended 2026-06 (`source_id: yyyyy`)
```

Also leave one line in `log`. Remove the block once a human resolves it.

Newer is not necessarily more correct. Casual conversation after minutes with confirmed values often relies on outdated assumptions.

### Citations

Every Wiki statement must be traceable to a source.

- Page level: add `source_id` to front matter `sources`.
- When sources differ by paragraph: add `(source: xxxxx)` in the body.
- Do not write information absent from `raw/` (your inference or general knowledge). If absolutely necessary, explicitly label it as "speculation".

## Sensitive information

Because the original text remains in `raw/`, the Wiki layer need not reproduce everything. Handle each category differently.

| Category | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Personal email addresses, phone numbers, and DM handles | **Do not write them in the body.** State only the method, such as “Contact via X DM,” and refer readers to `raw/` for the actual destination. |
| Amounts, unit prices, and estimates | **May be written.** They are needed for catering and venue recommendations. Keep page `visibility` at `restricted`. |
| Incidents | **Write only the event and prevention measure.** Do not name involved people; anonymize, for example, “one staff member.” |
| Personal evaluations and impressions | Do not write negative evaluations of identifiable individuals. Retain only content that can be generalized into an operational decision. |
| Credentials and API keys | Never write them. If found, record only “credentials present in `raw/`” in `log`. |

When uncertain, do not write it. Referring to `raw/` preserves it.

## `index` and `log`

**`index`** — Catalog of all pages. Split into sections by type, one line per page.

```markdown
## Venues

- [Umeda Innovation Hub](venues/umeda-innovation-hub) — Capacity 120. Equipment may be brought in. Used twice in 2026.
```

Always add an entry when creating a page. Remove it when deleting one.

**`log`** — Append-only chronological record. Do not rewrite existing lines.

```markdown
## [2026-08-10] ingest | July 14 regular meeting minutes

- Updated venues/umeda-innovation-hub (capacity, whether equipment can be brought in)
- Updated events/2026-07-18-io-extended-osaka (attendance)
- Contradiction: capacity 120 / 90. Added an Unresolved block to venues/umeda-innovation-hub.
```

The heading must always be `## [YYYY-MM-DD] <type> | <subject>`. Types are `ingest` / `lint` / `query`.
Do not alter this format: it is parsed with `grep "^## \\["`.

## Lint

When invoked by `gdg wiki lint`, check the following in order and record results in `log`. You may fix pages as well.

1. **Contradictions** — Whether different pages say different things about the same subject.
2. **Staleness** — Whether pages remain unupdated despite new sources.
3. **Orphans** — Pages not linked from anywhere.
4. **Missing entities** — Entities (venues, vendors, people) mentioned by multiple pages but lacking a page of their own.
5. **`index` synchronization** — Pages missing from the catalog or catalog entries with no page.
6. **Missing `summary`** — Empty summaries or summaries that conflict with the body.
7. **Missing citations** — Pages stating concrete facts while `sources` is empty.
8. **Sensitive information** — Content violating the table above.

For issues you cannot resolve yourself (contradictions requiring judgment or gaps requiring further research), leave a "Needs action" entry in `log`.

## Language

This clone is single-language. `page.md` contains only the language selected at clone time.

Other-language versions are generated automatically from Japanese by the server-side translation pipeline. Do not write translations.
Do not change `translation_status`, either.
````
